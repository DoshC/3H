First trip to Colombia?

What people ask before booking.

Is Colombia safe to visit right now?

The regions we operate in are monitored daily and chosen for their safety record. Every trip includes a local guide and a 24/7 emergency contact.

Do I need a visa?

Most US and EU citizens can enter visa-free for stays under 90 days with a valid passport. We send a personalized checklist after booking.

How do I plan my itinerary?

Start with one anchor region, then we help you sequence 2–3 more based on flight routes and season — most trips run 7–14 days.

What's the best budget to prepare?

Tours listed here cover guiding, transport, and activities; plan separately for flights, meals outside group activities, and personal spending.
